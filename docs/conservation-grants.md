# Conservation funding for the underground irrigation conversion

Researched 2026-08-24. Supersedes the rough notes in
`conservation-grants.txt` (kept for reference). **Correction to those
notes: COID itself does not appear to run a patron grant program** — COID's
money goes into its own large-scale canal piping (e.g. the $25M federally
funded Pilot Butte Canal project). What COID offers landowners directly is
a **low-interest loan program** ($400K over two years) and partnership in
the county on-farm program below. The actual on-farm *grants* come from
DSWCD/DRC, NRCS, and Energy Trust.

## Best fit first: Deschutes County On-Farm Efficiency Water Conservation Investment

Partnership of **Deschutes SWCD + Deschutes River Conservancy (DRC) + COID**,
originally seeded with **$1.45M of county ARPA funds**.

- **What it funds:** piping private laterals, upgrading on-farm efficiency
  infrastructure, flood→sprinkler conversion, "or other water conservation
  savings activities" — the underground-PVC conversion fits squarely.
- **Terms:** up to **$15,000 per landowner**, **1:1 cost-share** (you match).
- Leverages **$400,000 of COID investment** in a **low-interest loan
  program** for landowners — useful for the portion above the grant cap.
- 20% of funding is set aside for irrigators across Deschutes County and
  other irrigation districts.
- **Contacts:**
  - Erin Kilcullen (DSWCD) — Ekilcullen.dswcd@outlook.com
  - Kate Fitzpatrick (DRC) — kate@deschutesriver.org
  - Baxter Davies (COID) — bdavies@coid.org — **541-550-6834**
- ⚠️ Program status/remaining funds **not confirmed** (ARPA-seeded, 2021
  vintage) — ask whether it's still funded for 2026 when calling.

## NRCS EQIP — Deschutes County On-farm Irrigation and Energy Efficiency

USDA NRCS runs a **Deschutes-County-specific** EQIP initiative for on-farm
irrigation + energy efficiency (sprinkler irrigation and energy efficiency
improvements are explicitly in its prioritization questions).

- Applications accepted **continuously**, but ranked on **state-specific
  ranking dates** — you must apply before the cutoff to be in the current
  funding cycle. **The 2026 Oregon ranking date was not published in the
  sources found — confirm with the local field office.**
- Cost-share assistance for eligible agricultural producers; a local
  conservationist does a free site visit and helps build the application.
- ⚠️ Eligibility likely requires qualifying as an **agricultural producer**
  (farm number / ag use) — confirm; this may be the gating question for a
  property this size.
- Next step: contact the **Redmond/Deschutes County NRCS field office** for
  the ranking date and an eligibility check.

## Energy Trust of Oregon — equipment incentives (not a grant, but stackable)

Confirmed **2026 incentives are active**. Relevant to this project:

- **Sprinklers, nozzles and gaskets** — cash incentives (our aluminum
  latch pipe leaks at gaskets; new heads/nozzles qualify).
- **Irrigation system conversions** — e.g. **$500/acre** for hand line /
  solid set / traveler → **drip**.
- **Irrigation pump VFD** — incentives for VFDs on existing pumps **up to
  100 HP** (new retrofit option) and new pumps up to 22.5 HP. *A VFD on the
  5 HP pump is worth pricing — it would also solve the recycle/pressure
  problem in hardware.*
- **Sprinkler package upgrades** (LESA, LEPA, MESA, PMDI) — pivot-oriented,
  probably N/A here.
- **Eligibility:** Energy Trust serves customers of **Portland General,
  Pacific Power, NW Natural, Cascade Natural Gas** — confirm the pump's
  utility qualifies.
- ⚠️ **Do not buy equipment before checking pre-approval requirements** —
  most incentive programs require application *before* purchase/install.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Call COID first: 541-548-6047** (or Baxter Davies, 541-550-6834). Ask
   (a) is the county on-farm efficiency cost-share still funded for 2026,
   (b) is this property on a lateral scheduled for district piping — that
   often unlocks dedicated funding, (c) the low-interest loan terms.
2. **Contact Redmond/Deschutes NRCS field office** — free site visit, EQIP
   eligibility check, and the 2026 ranking date.
3. **Check Energy Trust pre-approval** before purchasing any pipe/heads/VFD.
4. Stack where allowed: EQIP or county cost-share for the piping + Energy
   Trust for equipment + COID loan for the remainder.
